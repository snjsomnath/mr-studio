### MR Studio's own About window

About MR Studio (in the app menu on a Mac, and under Help on Windows) opens a light window in place of the plain system box. Clicking the logo on Home opens it too. It shows the logo, the version and build, and the computer's system. It also has a link to what is new in this version, who made MR Studio, the artists' credits, where the map data comes from and every open-source library with its licence. A table's own credits stay on its controller.
