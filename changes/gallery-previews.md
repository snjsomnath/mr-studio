### The New layer gallery shows a picture of more layers

Base map, Sight lines, Magnifier, Swipe, Slideshow, Masterplan, Picture or video and Sketch now show a picture of what they draw in the New layer gallery, made from the preview site by `scripts/layer-previews.cjs`. Web map, Video walk, Field walk and layers you make yourself still show their icon, because they need a service, a video or a walk to draw anything.
