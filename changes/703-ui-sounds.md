### Interface sounds on the table

The table now plays a soft tap when a layer button is pressed on the controller and a short chime when it opens for presenting, so a visitor across the room hears that the press worked. The sounds are made in the app, play only on the table's speakers (never in the editor), stay quiet while the table resets, and the controller's sound switch silences them. Their volume is Off, Soft or Normal under Present › Screens › Interface sounds; Soft is the default.
