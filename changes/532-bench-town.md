### `mrstudio bench` prints one line per kernel and can time a city-sized town

`mrstudio bench` now prints one line per kernel (sunlight at noon, at a low sun, a Sun hours day, wind and stormwater) with its time, its grid and the backend, so two computers can be compared line by line (#532). `--town 4000` times a made-up town of 4 by 4 km at 1 m cells at the site's place instead of the site itself, for the city-sized measurement.
