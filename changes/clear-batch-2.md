### A new version waits for the show, and phones can look when the table is full

Opening a newer version of a table that is on the screens now offers "Update when the show stops". The file waits, Home says so, and it goes in place when someone stops presenting or at the nightly restart. "Stop and update now" does it at once. A phone that joins when every seat is taken can still look around, and gets a seat when one frees up. In Sketch, a phone can add a few words to its last mark. The table shows only a small ring, and the controller shows the words once "Show what the phones said" is on.
