### A setup that looks like MR Studio

The Windows setup has MR Studio's light look: a picture of the table on the Welcome page, a different one on the Finish page, and while the files copy, a slideshow of what the table can do (sun and shadow, wind, cool paths, trams and buses, rain, places), made from real table pictures. It is sharp at 100, 150 and 200 % screen scaling. The setup no longer shows its list of copied files.

On the Mac, the .dmg window has the same look, with an arrow from the app to Applications.
