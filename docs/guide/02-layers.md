# Add and set up layers

*Put layers on the table, give each one a button, and choose which controls visitors see.*

1. Open a table in the editor. The list on the left has **Map** (what is on the table at rest), **Layers** (what the controller's buttons open) and **Controller**.
2. On the **Edit** tab of the ribbon, under **Add a layer**, pick a layer. It goes where its kind belongs: life on the streets under Map, analyses under Layers.
3. Select the layer in the list. The middle shows it live on the map, and the panel on the right has its settings: the time of day for sunlight, the storm for stormwater, and so on.
4. Under **Button**, set the name, a short description and an icon, and the side of the controller it sits on. A **group** puts related buttons together; **Move up** and **Move down** change the order.
5. Under the controls, untick the ones visitors should not change. They keep their value; only the controller and phones leave them out.
6. A layer can hold **items** that show only while it is open, for example public transport while the sun study is on. Add them with the layer selected.
7. **Show** and **Hide** decide whether something is on at rest. **Remove** takes it out of the table.
8. Check the result with **Preview**, or open **Map** and **Controller** in windows of their own from the ribbon.
