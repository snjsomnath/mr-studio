# Displays, colour and projector calibration

*Make the projection land on the model and look right.*

1. On the editor's **Present** tab, open **Screens and calibration…**. These settings belong to this computer, so they are the same for every table on it. The dialog sits under the ribbon so the map stays in view.
2. **Screens**: choose which display is the table (the projector) and which is the controller. **Identify** shows a number on each screen.
3. **Calibrate** shows a cross of light on the table. The panel that goes with it is in the middle of the controller and in the editor's calibration guide: it names the corner to find and shows it on a small plan. Move the cross onto that corner of the physical model (drag it, or use the nudge pad on a touch screen), then **Next**; **Back** and **Skip** move between corners, **Save** keeps the result. The table itself shows only the cross and the corners already placed, so nothing covers the model. With no controller screen, press K on the table to show the panel there.
   - With building footprints, the corners to find are real corners of buildings, at their roof height, outlined on the plan. Without footprints, MR Studio picks corners from the ground heights.
4. **Colour**: boost saturation, contrast and brightness for each display, set midtones and white balance, and save it as a preset. A test card helps.
5. **Layer strength** dims or brightens a single layer on the projector only.
6. **Quality**: Draft keeps a slow computer smooth, Best gives a strong one finer detail.
