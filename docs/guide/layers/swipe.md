# Reveal line

## What it shows

A glowing line across the table that visitors drag. On one side of it the layers you placed inside it show, such as pipes, tunnels and the metro under the ground; on the other the table stays as it is.

![The Reveal line over what is under the ground](docs/screenshots/288-reveal-line.png)

## Good for

Comparing what is above and below ground, then and now, or a plan against today, by sweeping between them.

## Set it up

1. Add **Reveal line** from **Add a layer**.
2. Add the layers to reveal inside it in the layer list (for example a Masterplan with an underground drawing).
3. Choose how **The line runs** and, under more settings, which side **The layers inside show** on.
4. Drag the line on the table (a press anywhere jumps it there), on the controller's table picture, or with a phone's Move the line tool.

## Tips

- Turn on **The line sweeps slowly while nobody moves it** to have it invite people in when the table is idle.
- **Hide the map where they show** gives the revealed side a dark background.
- For then and now, put two layers inside (say the 1960 aerial photo, then today's) and turn on **With two layers inside, the second shows on the other side**.
