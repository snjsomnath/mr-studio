# Street glow

## What it shows

The streets glowing, a colour for each kind of street (main roads, local streets, paths), pulsing gently, with lights flowing along them.

![Glowing streets](docs/screenshots/195-close.png)

## Good for

A striking look at rest, and showing the street hierarchy at a glance.

## Set it up

1. The table needs its streets: Insert tab, **Data file**, **Streets**, **From OpenStreetMap**.
2. Add **Street glow** from **Add a layer**.
3. Set **Brightness** and **Pulse**. **Lights flowing, per kilometre** sets how many lights travel along the streets.

## Tips

- With **Reveal one kind of street at a time when it opens**, the main roads light up first, then the smaller streets.
- Use it on its own with a dark base map; together with Traffic it can get busy.
