# Slideshow

## What it shows

A sequence of slides shown on the table one after another: layers, pictures, videos, map data and web maps. The controller shows each slide's title, text and legend. A slide that shows a layer of the table, such as a Magnifier X-ray, Sunlight or a custom layer, keeps the base map under it and brings that layer's own controls onto the controller (the glass's navigator, the time slider), as when it is opened from its button. Pictures, videos and web maps fill the table on their own.

![Slides in the editor](docs/screenshots/43-slides-editor.png)

## Good for

Telling a story in steps, and guided tours where a guide moves on with Next.

## Set it up

1. Add **Slideshow** from **Insert › New layer** and select it.
2. Press **Add a slide** and choose what it shows: a layer in the table, a picture or video, map data, or a web map.
3. Give each slide a title and text for the controller, and notes for the guide.
4. Choose how each slide moves on: by hand with Next, or after some seconds.
5. Rehearse with **Try Next** in the editor.

## Tips

- The step-by-step guide "Make a slideshow or a guided tour" has the full walkthrough, including running it from a guide's phone.
