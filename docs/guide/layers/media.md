# Picture or video

## What it shows

A picture or video laid over the whole table: a simulation made in another program, an old photo, a film of a sun study.

![A picture over the table](docs/screenshots/197-table-picture.png)

## Good for

Showing results from other tools on the model, and before-and-after pictures.

## Set it up

1. Make the picture or video with north up, at the table's shape if you can.
2. Add **Picture or video** from **Insert › New layer**, or add it as a slide in a slideshow.
3. Under **File**, choose the picture (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF) or video (MP4, WebM).
4. Under **Fit to the table**, choose stretch, the whole picture with margins, or fill and crop. **Turn** rotates it a quarter at a time.

## Tips

- Videos play without sound and start again when they end (unless you turn that off).
- In a slideshow, slides can move on with a cut, a fade or a dip to black.
