# Cool paths

## What it shows

Two walks between the same two points: the shortest one, and the one with the least heat, which is a little longer. Every path and the ground are coloured by how hot it feels (PET, "felt temperature"), and two walkers set off together so visitors see what the detour costs.

![Cool and short walks](docs/screenshots/196-coolpaths-pet-table.jpg)

## Good for

Heat on summer days, the value of shade and trees, and planning walks for people who suffer in the heat.

## Set it up

1. The table needs its buildings and its walking paths. In **Add data**, choose **Walking paths** and fetch them from OpenStreetMap.
2. Add **Cool paths** from **Add a layer**.
3. Set the **Date** and **Time of day**. A hot afternoon in July shows the biggest difference.
4. **Longest detour** sets how much longer the cool walk may be (50% means half as long again).
5. Choose **Start of the walk** and **End of the walk** on a phone, or click two points on the table. **Try another walk** picks two for you.

## Tips

- The controller compares the two walks: distance, average felt temperature and total heat on the way.
- The weather settings (air temperature, humidity, wind) are under **More settings**.
