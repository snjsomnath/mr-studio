# Sketch

## What it shows

Marks visitors draw from their phones: places, routes and areas, each phone in its own colour.

## Good for

Workshops and dialogue: "show us where you walk", "mark where it feels unsafe".

## Set it up

1. Add **Sketch** from **Add a layer**.
2. Visitors join with their phones by scanning the code on the controller (guide "Set up the controller, languages and phones").
3. On the phone, they choose **Place**, **Route** or **Area** and draw on the map.
4. **Clear the sketch** removes all marks when the workshop is over.

## Tips

- Very old marks drop off after a while, so a long day stays readable.
