# Sketch

## What it shows

Marks visitors draw from their phones: places, pen strokes, arrows, routes and areas, each phone in its own colour. Where a mark lands, the table shows the phone's animal for a moment.

## Good for

Workshops and dialogue: "show us where you walk", "mark where it feels unsafe".

## Set it up

1. Add **Sketch** from **Insert › New layer**.
2. Visitors join with their phones by scanning the code on the controller (guide "Set up the controller, languages and phones").
3. On the phone they open **Sketch**, choose **Place**, **Pen**, **Arrow**, **Route** or **Area** and draw on the map. Two fingers move and zoom the map; the corner button opens it full screen.
4. **Take back my last mark** on a phone removes that phone's own last mark.
5. **Clear the sketch** removes all marks when the workshop is over. **End the round** in the Developer window's Phones panel lets every phone go and clears the sketch too.

## Tips

- Very old marks drop off after a while, so a long day stays readable.
