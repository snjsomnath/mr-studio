# Street life

## What it shows

People walking on the footways and paths, cyclists on the cycle lanes, and warm streetlights along the main streets.

![Street life at rest](docs/screenshots/195-at-rest.png)

## Good for

Making the table feel inhabited when nobody is using it, especially together with Traffic and Building lights.

## Set it up

1. The table needs its streets, including footways and cycleways: Edit tab, **Add data**, **Streets**, **From OpenStreetMap**.
2. Add **Street life** from **Add a layer**. It goes under **Map**.
3. Set **Walkers per kilometre of path** and **Cyclists per kilometre of cycleway**.

## Tips

- **Pace** above 1 speeds people up so the movement reads from a distance.
- Turn on **Streetlights on side streets too** for an evening look.
