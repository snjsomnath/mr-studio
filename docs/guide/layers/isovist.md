# Isovist

## What it shows

What a person standing at one spot can see: the view is drawn in yellow out to the buildings and trees it reaches. The controller shows Street View from that spot, how green the view is, and plays what the place sounds like.

![What a person here can see](docs/screenshots/190-isovist-table.png)

## Good for

Views and sightlines, how open or closed a square feels, and how much greenery people actually see.

## Set it up

1. The table needs its buildings (and trees, if they should block the view): Edit tab, **Add data**.
2. Add **Isovist** from **Add a layer**.
3. Click on the table to stand somewhere. The viewer looks towards the pointer and walks after it. On a phone, use **Where you stand** and **Where you look**.
4. Choose a **Field of view**: a person's 120°, or all round.

## Tips

- Street View on the controller needs a Google key under **Sources and keys** on Home. Without one, the rest works.
- The green view rating counts the share of the view that ends in trees: 30% is good, 15% fair.
