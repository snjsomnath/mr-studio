# Magnifier

## What it shows

A round glass that visitors move over the table. It shows what is under it larger, or, as an X-ray, the layers you placed inside it (pipes under the street, an old map, a plan) only inside the glass.

![The Magnifier as an X-ray](docs/screenshots/287-xray.png)

## Good for

Looking closely at one part of a detailed drawing, and revealing what lies under the ground or behind today's city, one spot at a time.

## Set it up

1. Add **Magnifier** from **Add a layer**.
2. For a magnifier, leave **The glass shows** on *The table, larger* and set **Magnification**.
3. For an X-ray, set **The glass shows** to *The layers inside it (an X-ray)*, then add the layers to show inside the Magnifier in the layer list (for example a Masterplan with an underground drawing).
4. On the table the glass follows the mouse; a click keeps it where it is. On the controller, drag the glass on the table picture. Phones get a Move the glass tool.

## Tips

- **Darken the table around the glass** draws eyes to it; turn it down for a subtler look.
- **Hide the map inside the glass** gives an X-ray a dark background, so what is inside reads clearly.
- The larger view is a copy of the table's picture, so it is softer on a plain screen than on the projector at full size.
