# Set up the controller, languages and phones

*What visitors see on the screen beside the table and on their phones.*

1. Open **Design, Welcome and languages…**. Set the controller's title, a logo (pick one of the table's pictures, or **Add a picture**; it shows above the title on the welcome screen and beside it on phones) and the **welcome line** under the title on the home screen.
2. The controller's **theme** and **button style** are on the ribbon's **Design** tab. Each button's words, icon and side are in the Button section of its layer's settings.
3. While nobody touches it, the controller's home screen loops through the layers by itself and returns home after a quiet spell. Set how long in the same dialog.
4. **Languages for visitors**: tick English and Swedish to give the controller and phones a language switch. The first one is where the table starts. Translate the table's own words (title, welcome line, button names, slide titles) in the list below.
5. **Phones**: visitors scan the code on the controller. On the table's Wi-Fi they join directly; from any other network they join through the relay. A phone lists every layer; tapping one opens its page with its switch, its controls and, for layers you draw on, a map. **Phones can switch layers** in Table settings decides whether visitors may turn layers on.
6. **Two layers at once**: a plain press on a layer button opens that layer alone. Hold a button until the ring round it closes (or Shift-click it with a mouse) to add it to the open layer, such as Wind over Sunlight. At most two are open; a third replaces the older. The controller shows the newest one's controls and a chip for the other: press the chip to swap, or its × to close it. The slideshow, Picture or video and the video walk always open alone. Turn it off with **Two layers at once** in the same dialog.
7. **Looking after the phones** during a session: the Developer window's **Phones** panel pauses every phone, lets one go, ends the round (every phone goes and the sketch is cleared) and downloads a log of what the phones did.
8. **Ribbons**: if the projector covers more than the model, set which edge visitors stand at in **Design, Table size and place…**, and let the ribbons use the blank space for at-a-glance information.
