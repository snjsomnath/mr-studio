# Make a slideshow or a guided tour

*A sequence of layers, pictures and maps that Next moves through. With notes, a guide runs it from their phone.*

1. Add the **Slideshow** layer and select it.
2. **Add a slide** for each step: a layer already in the table, a new one only for this slideshow, a picture or video, map data from the site, or a web map (WMS).
3. For each slide, choose how it moves on (**Manual** or after a time) and what **Next** does: go to the next slide, or reveal a map's categories one by one first.
4. Write **notes** on each step. They show on the guide's phone and in the editor, never on the table or the visitors' controller.
5. Choose **Run it from the guide's phone**. Scan the code with your phone: it shows the step, your notes, and Next and Previous.
6. Use **Try Next** in the editor to rehearse.
